# Code Of Conduct

Synap follows the Contributor Covenant spirit: participate with respect, assume good faith, and make technical disagreement precise rather than personal.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, kind, and specific.
- Critique designs and code, not people.
- Document decisions so future contributors can understand tradeoffs.
- Welcome cautious questions about correctness, safety, and scope.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks in design discussions.
- Publishing private security details without coordination.
- Repeated disruption of maintainer processes.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; pause discussions; or restrict participation when behavior harms the project community.
